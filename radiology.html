Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions our patients ask about radiology tests, scans, and imaging procedures.

Most radiology tests are painless. X-Rays, Ultrasound, and Doppler studies do not cause pain. Mammography may cause mild temporary discomfort due to compression.

Most scans are quick. X-Rays take only a few minutes, while Ultrasound, Doppler, and Mammography usually take 15–30 minutes.

Preparation depends on the type of scan. Some Ultrasound tests may require fasting or a full bladder, while X-Rays and Mammography need minimal preparation.

Yes, these imaging techniques are generally safe when performed under medical supervision. X-Rays use low-dose radiation, while Ultrasound and Doppler use sound waves.

Reports are typically available on the same day or within 24 hours, depending on the scan and evaluation.

Mammography plays a crucial role in early detection of breast conditions, especially for women above 40 years.
